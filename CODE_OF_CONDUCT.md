# Code of Conduct

RAY aims to maintain a professional, technical, and constructive collaboration environment.

Participants are expected to:

- discuss technical disagreements on their merits;
- provide reproducible examples when reporting bugs;
- avoid harassment, personal attacks, and discriminatory conduct;
- respect authorship, licensing, and citation requirements;
- keep issue and pull-request discussions relevant to the project.

Maintainers may moderate or remove interactions that materially disrupt technical collaboration.
